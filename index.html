What's Holding Erickson Living Back

Five root-cause challenges fragmenting procurement, eroding margins, and consuming the staff time that should go to resident care.

Challenge 01
Procurement Fragmented Across 20+ Communities
Each community sources independently with no centralized catalog, pricing, or visibility — making spend governance impossible at the enterprise level.
"Legacy sites convert 45% below Salesforce-powered benchmarks."
Challenge 02
Staff Time Consumed by Manual Ordering Processes
Community managers and procurement staff spend hours each week on manual order entry, approval workflows, and vendor coordination that could be automated.
"Without self-service, reps spend their selling time processing routine orders."
Challenge 03
Custom Platform Can't Enforce Contract Pricing
Preferred vendor pricing and contract terms aren't enforced at the point of order — resulting in off-contract spend and measurable revenue leakage.
"Organizations pay $473K+ over 3 years keeping legacy systems alive."
Challenge 04
No Centralized Catalog or Spend Visibility
Finance and operations leadership cannot see what's being ordered, by whom, or at what price, across communities — making budget management reactive.
"Teams spend 2× as long on maintenance vs. innovation on legacy platforms."
Challenge 05
Returns and Exchanges Require Manual CS Involvement
Every return, exchange, or order correction requires staff involvement — no self-service options exist for community managers, creating unnecessary support burden.
"Agents spend 40% more time per case without a unified order screen."

Five real operational changes that Erickson Living teams will feel from day one — freeing staff to focus on care, not procurement friction.

Before
Community managers call vendors for routine supply reorders — consuming hours each week in back-and-forth calls and emails.
After
Self-service portal handles all standard reorders automatically — one-click reorder from order history, no calls required.
Before
Off-contract purchasing drains budget across communities with no way to enforce preferred vendor pricing at the point of order.
After
Contract pricing enforced at point of order, every time — account entitlements ensure staff only see and pay contract rates.
Before
Finance can't see community-level spend until month-end close — making in-flight budget management impossible.
After

After
Self-service return initiation, status tracking, and confirmation — resolved in minutes, no phone calls needed.

Branded procurement portal deployed in 30 minutes, no IT required — new communities inherit all catalogs, pricing, and approvals immediately.
